About Surbhi

Surbhi is the founder of Mohini Mantra- A private healing and inner transformation space designed for those who require depth, discretion, and true understanding. Having navigated her own intimate journey with mental health, Surbhi brings a rare blend of lived experience, emotional intelligence, and intuitive mastery to her work. Her approach is neither purely clinical nor purely spiritual it is integrative, precise, and deeply human. She works with individuals who are often high-functioning, sensitive, and perceptive.People whose inner complexity is frequently misunderstood or overlooked. Surbhi’s work honors the reality that emotional distress cannot be resolved through surface-level tools alone. Healing requires presence, safety, nervous system attunement, and a soul-aware perspective. Mohini Mantra is built on the philosophy that true transformation is not dramatic - it is subtle, embodied, and enduring.
